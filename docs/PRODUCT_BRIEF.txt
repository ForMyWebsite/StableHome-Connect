MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT: STABLEHOME CONNECT
Project Identity
Build a complete, professional, mobile-first web application called StableHome Connect.

Tagline: Prevent eviction. Find assistance. Stay housed.

Product category: Housing stability, eviction prevention, civic technology, and community assistance.

Initial geographic focus: Southeastern Pennsylvania, beginning with Delaware County and Philadelphia, with an architecture that supports expanding into additional counties and states.

Primary objective: Build an accessible digital platform that helps tenants facing potential housing instability identify assistance programs, organize documents, understand relevant processes, explore repayment options, and coordinate voluntary dispute resolution with landlords and qualified housing professionals.

Important: This is an independent civic-technology prototype inspired by the goals of eviction diversion programs and the proposed Stable Homes Act. It is not an official government portal, legal service, court system, financial institution, or benefits eligibility authority.

The application must be functional, responsive, secure by design, and suitable for presentation at the Congressional App Challenge.

Avoid creating a superficial visual mockup. Build actual working functionality with persistent data, meaningful algorithmic features, navigation, authentication, form validation, state management, and a complete demonstration workflow.

Matching Algorithm
Implement a score-based recommendation algorithm.

Begin with jurisdiction filtering.

Exclude programs that are confirmed not to serve the user's jurisdiction.

Then calculate a relevance score from 0 to 100.

Suggested weights:

Geographic match: 30 points

Service category match: 25 points

Published eligibility alignment: 20 points

Urgency appropriateness: 15 points

Language/accessibility match: 10 points

Unknown eligibility should not automatically disqualify users.

For example:

A tenant in Delaware County experiencing overdue rent would likely receive higher matches for resources serving Delaware County that provide rental assistance.

A resource serving only Philadelphia would generally not be presented as a local match for a Delaware County resident unless its official service area includes them.

Algorithm Requirements
Explain why each resource matches.

Do not fabricate eligibility information.

Do not fabricate available funding.

Do not claim application acceptance unless confirmed.

Display a confidence or completeness indicator based on available program information.

Handle missing information gracefully.

Rank results deterministically.

Support filters and sorting.

Provide links to official sources.

Save the user's selected resources.

The ranking score measures relevance, not probability of acceptance.

SECTION 8: REPAYMENT AND AFFORDABILITY PLANNER
Create /planner.

This feature helps users visualize possible repayment schedules.

Required Inputs
Rent arrears being discussed

Proposed initial payment

Remaining payment count

Payment interval

Expected monthly available amount

Optional future rent obligation

Core Calculation
Remaining balance = Total arrears - Initial payment

Installment amount = Remaining balance / Number of installments

Calculate all financial values using integer cents or a decimal-safe currency library.

Distribute any rounding difference across installments while preserving the exact total.

Affordability Estimate
Available amount = Monthly income - Essential monthly expenses - Current rent obligation

Compare proposed monthly repayment obligations against this estimated amount.

Show:

Proposed payment

Estimated remaining household funds

Warning if obligations exceed the user's estimate

Remaining balance over time

Do not describe a proposed plan as affordable, safe, legally enforceable, or financially appropriate based solely on a mathematical result.
