# Resource source review — October 25, 2026

These are informational pathways, not confirmed funding or eligibility. The dataset keeps unknown income criteria, appointment requirements, and language availability empty. `last_verified_at` records a source-content review, not individual service acceptance or available funding. Administrators should recheck listings regularly and after source changes.

No ZIP-to-county dataset is imported. Users confirm county or equivalent explicitly, because postal ZIPs can overlap jurisdictions. Local listings require that confirmation. National finders remain available for all states and territories; this is not an assertion that every program or county has been cataloged.

SSI, SNAP, and housing benefits education link separately to [SSA](https://www.ssa.gov/ssi/), [USDA](https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/recipient/eligibility), and [HUD](https://www.hud.gov/). The app does not merge their rules or claim that proposed legislation has become law.
